About Us

Born in Phoenix, AZ and forged in the gray skies of Seattle since 2013, Sick of mE is the sound of monotony, madness, and everything in between. A one-man project that blurs the line between rock, punk, and metal, Sick of mE channels raw emotion into riffs that are heavy but never overbearing, melodic yet jagged, cathartic without pretense.

The lyrics lean self-deprecating, circling around themes of death, decay, and the surreal comedy of suburban dystopia. It’s the soundtrack to feeling stuck in loops you can’t escape, staring down the absurdity of modern life while laughing through the spiral.

Musically, Sick of mE avoids the clichés of extreme metal—no blast beats, no throat-shredding growls—just a gritty fusion that lives somewhere between the alternative edge of Smashing Pumpkins, the intensity of Slipknot, and the bittersweet weight of what could only be described as emo dad rock.
